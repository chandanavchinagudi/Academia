# SkillBridge 🚀

### Bridging Talent, Academia & Industry

SkillBridge is a web-based platform developed as a prototype for the **Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2026**. It aims to bridge the gap between students, academic institutions, and industries by connecting skill assessment, skill-gap analysis, internships, placements, and career opportunities in one platform.

---

## 📌 Problem

There is a gap between the skills students acquire through academics and the skills required by industries.

Students often face difficulties in:

- Understanding which skills are required for their target career
- Identifying their current skill gaps
- Finding relevant internships and job opportunities
- Maintaining their skills, certifications, and achievements in one place
- Tracking their applications

At the same time, colleges need better visibility into student skill levels, while industries need an easier way to identify candidates with relevant skills.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

SkillBridge provides a unified platform connecting:

**👨‍🎓 Students ↔ 🏫 Academia ↔ 🏢 Industry**

The platform helps students:

- Create a skill-based profile
- Take skill assessments
- Identify skill gaps
- Explore internships and job opportunities
- Apply for opportunities
- Track application status
- Maintain a digital skill profile

The platform also provides concepts for college and industry dashboards to support skill development, recruitment, and collaboration.
